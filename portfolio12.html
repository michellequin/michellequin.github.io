TikTok Ads Performance

Bridging the TikTok vs. Meta Ad Spend Gap: Stability, Trustworthiness, and Automation

I'm currently the lead horizontal researcher on TikTok's Global Monetization Product Technology team, focusing on advertiser and consumer ads experiences in North America and Europe. The big issue I'm tasked with researching: the TikTok vs. Meta user monetary spend gap. From data, we know the average TikTok user spends significantly more time per session scrolling on TikTok than the average Instagram user does scrolling on Instagram, and yet, users spend much more money per session on Instagram than on TikTok. How can we improve ads performance on TikTok?

So far, I've done mixed methods studies targeting advertiser perceptions and experiences on TikTok, and user perceptions of TikTok ads vs. Meta ads, tackling a fundamental problem with platform trustworthiness and reputation. I've also worked to improve automation for advertiser tooling and ad performance stability, addressing the frustration that TikTok has faster growth than Meta but inconsistent results. My work has resulted in direct changes to platform-side advertisement and notification wording/framing, advertiser-side insights/reporting tooling, advertisement bidding strategy and spend type prioritization, and more.

Methods used

  • Surveys (Qualtrics, IBM SPSS, Google Suite)
  • User Interviews (Zoom, Calendly)
  • Synthesis Reports
  • Competitor Analysis
  • Internationalization (AI translation tools)
